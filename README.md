# Ember Pact v3 — Oath, stance and tell: a second decision every hand

A duel of the four winds against Rook the Cinder Fox. You and Rook have 30 life each. Play trick-taking duels with a 36-card elemental deck plus 4 Pact wilds.

## How it plays

- Four elements — Ember, Gale, Tide, Stone — on a wheel: Ember beats Gale, Gale beats Tide, Tide beats Ember; Stone is neutral.
- Each hand, swear an **Oath** on one element: +2 power to its cards all hand, but lose the hand and the broken Oath costs you +3 damage.
- Each hand Rook favours one element (+1 power), shown in a banner — honest about 70% of the time.
- Before every trick, Rook's **intent tell** names the element it eyes — counter it if you believe it (~70% true).
- Win a trick to deal damage equal to the power difference (min 1). Win two tricks in a row to arm **Surge** (+2 on the next win). Win with the same element twice running for a **Chain** bonus (+1).
- Pact wilds let you pick their element when played.
- First to 0 life loses the hand; take the most hands to win the duel.

## Play it

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser, or run `node serve.ts` and play at http://localhost:8080. Mouse or keyboard (1–5 play a card).
