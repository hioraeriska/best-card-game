A DUEL OF CARDS AND CUNNING

EMBER PACT

Duel of the Four Winds

Beat Rook the Cinder Fox. You both start at 30 life.

Each trick, play one card from your hand. The higher power wins; the loser loses life equal to the difference (at least 1 on a win; a tie is a clash).

Ember beats Gale · Gale beats Tide · Tide beats Ember · Stone is neutral. A winning element adds +3 power. Pact wilds (value 6) become the element that beats your rival’s card.

Win two tricks in a row to arm Surge — your next win deals +2 damage. Rook telegraphs a hint before you play: it tells the truth most of the time.

Click a card to select, click again to play · Keys 1–5 select · Enter plays · R restarts
